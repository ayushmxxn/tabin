# Privacy Policy for Tabin

**Last Updated:** September 2026

Your data stays yours. Tabin is designed to keep your personal information private and on your device.

---

## 1. Your Data

Your shortcuts, folders, Spaces, settings, wallpapers, and saved tabs are stored on your device:

- **Shortcuts, Folders, and Spaces:** Saved directly in your browser's local storage.
- **Settings & Preferences:** Layout options, theme preferences, and configurations stay on your computer.
- **Wallpapers:** Bundled presets are included with the extension. Custom image wallpapers and live video files are kept on your device and are never uploaded.
- **Saved Tabs:** Tab sessions you choose to capture are saved locally to allow you to restore tabs and tab groups later.
- **No Cloud Sync:** Tabin does not create user accounts or sync your data to any cloud servers.

---

## 2. Websites

When you add a website, Tabin may connect to it to retrieve its title, icon, or preview image:

- **Metadata & Previews:** Tabin connects directly to the bookmarked website to retrieve its public title and preview image (`og:image`). Downloaded preview images are compressed and stored locally on your device.
- **Icons:** If a website does not provide an icon, Tabin requests the site's public icon from Google's favicon service using that site's address.
- **Direct Connections:** These requests are sent directly between your browser and the respective websites with no referrer headers.

---

## 3. Privacy

Tabin does not track your browsing or log your searches:

- **No Browsing History:** Tabin does not track or record the websites you visit.
- **No Search Logging:** Searching in Tabin filters your saved shortcuts locally on your device. Search queries are never sent to external servers.
- **No Analytics:** Tabin contains no tracking scripts, telemetry, or analytics cookies.
- **No Data Sales:** Tabin does not collect, monetize, or sell user data.

---

## 4. Managing Your Data

You maintain full control over your data:

- **Export:** You can export your shortcuts and workspace configuration at any time from **Settings → Import & Export**.
- **Clear Data:** You can delete all local data at any time from **Settings → Privacy → Clear local data** or by uninstalling the extension.
